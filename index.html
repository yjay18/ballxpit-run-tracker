Evolutions

How evolutions work

  • Both ingredient balls must be level 3, then combine them in the Fusion Reactor building.
  • Ingredient order never matters for evolutions.
  • If two balls CAN evolve together, the Fusion Reactor forces the evolution; you cannot arbitrary-fuse that pair (exception: the three Nosferatu components can also fuse pairwise).
  • Evolved balls can themselves be leveled to 3 and evolved again (Tier 2) or fused freely with any non-matching ball.
  • Passives evolve the same way but cannot be arbitrarily fused, only evolved via exact recipes.
